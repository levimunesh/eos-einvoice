eos e-invoice — third-party components and rule sets

Rule sets (converted to lookup tables at build time; content unchanged)
- EN 16931 validation artefacts 1.3.16, CEN/TC 434 (ConnectingEurope/eInvoicing-EN16931). Licence: EUPL 1.2.
- XRechnung Schematron 2.6.0 for XRechnung 3.0.2, KoSIT (itplr-kosit/xrechnung-schematron). Licence: Apache 2.0.
- Peppol BIS Billing 3.0.21 rules included by the XRechnung build, OpenPeppol.
- Severity levels from the KoSIT validator configuration for XRechnung, release 2026-08-31. Licence: Apache 2.0.
- XML Schemas: UN/CEFACT Cross Industry Invoice D16B (subset used by EN 16931) and OASIS UBL 2.1.
- France: BR-FR rules, Flux 2, Schematron 1.4.0.04 for CII and UBL (September 2026), by Quentin Houard and Cyrille
  Sautereau for FNFE-MPE. Licence: European Union Public Licence (EUPL).
- Australia / New Zealand: Peppol PINT A-NZ billing rules 1.1.3 (shared PINT rules and jurisdiction-aligned rules),
  OpenPeppol with the ATO and MBIE.
- Poland: FA(3) XML schema, version 1-0E, with its base type schemas, Ministerstwo Finansów (CIRFMF/ksef-docs).
  Licence of the repository: MIT.
- Canada: no rule set exists. The invoice information checks follow CRA guide RC4022 and are this tool's own.
